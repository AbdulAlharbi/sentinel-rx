# JUDGING.md — criterion → concrete evidence

Every claim points at a file, a command, or a number produced by a run in this repository.
Held-out numbers come from `results/summary.md` (regenerated by `make report`); `[hx]`
placeholders are replaced by the final held-out values in the last commit of P8.
